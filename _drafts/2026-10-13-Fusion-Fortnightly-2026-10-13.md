# Fusion Fortnightly | 2026-10-12

No fluff, all facts: Type One Energy raises $200 million. General Fusion reaches 1 keV on LM26. Germany launches a €500 million fusion milestone program. CFS orders more than 10,000 km of superconducting tape for ARC. Helical Fusion raises another ¥3.23 billion. STEP unveils its latest power plant design.

## Companies, financing, and commercial development

[Type One Energy closes a $200 million Series B](https://typeoneenergy.com/insights-announcements/type-one-energy-completes-200m-series-b-financing/). Total investment in Type One now exceeds $400 million. The funding supports Infinity One, its engineering prototype in Tennessee, but certainly isn't enough to get to first plasma.

[General Fusion reports LM26 plasma temperatures above 1 keV](https://generalfusion.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Thomson-Scattering-Measurement-of-1-keV-Electron-Temperature-on-Lawson-Machine-26.pdf). Lawson Machine 26 achieved electron temperatures exceeding 1 keV (12 million °C) through compression, with meaningful ion heating also reported. The results were co-published with UKAEA and measured using Thomson scattering diagnostics. This is a significant intermediate milestone for General Fusion's magnetized target fusion approach, though still short of its next goals of 10 keV and satisfying the Lawson criterion. It does not establish fusion breakeven or net energy production.

[General Fusion measures electron temperatures above 1 keV on LM26](https://generalfusion.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Thomson-Scattering-Measurement-of-1-keV-Electron-Temperature-on-Lawson-Machine-26.pdf). Thomson scattering measured a peak local electron temperature of 1.09 keV, up from 0.72 keV reported in July, aided by a new diagnostic view closer to the plasma core. A [companion paper](https://generalfusion.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Compressional-heating-above-1-keV-on-the-LM26-magnetized-target-fusion-machine.pdf) reports a later peak of 1.18 keV from AXUV measurements and neutron-inferred ion heating from 323 eV to 459 eV. The improved performance is attributed to better electron energy confinement, although plasma temperature still collapses at a compression ratio of 2.7. See the [Fusion Conclusion's July claim check](https://www.fusionconclusion.com/Claim-Check-2026-07-02/) for analysis of the earlier results. An interesting first data point for public market reactions to company-defined milestones: [GFUZ](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/GFUZ/?guccounter=1&guce_referrer=aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuZ29vZ2xlLmNvbS8&guce_referrer_sig=AQAAAEzLBq6SmJDjFniMHH97hw_68ThzDEEi4pHutBcCuQlZjMGM-1VlBB3Hf6TEAlbW_akNGsZHJqeFCS4uDbKjFDHEZa_eeM5zihv4KbYwGa9IO1f7rjbb_YLSq7iHY4a09RFZRyoP2LU4aOrLFN5uLrk5sOp3AdAV2OUwwg1rN0qv) trading volume increased 5-10 times the previous weeks and the price transiently went up about 10% but is still well within the scatter of the last month or so. It looks like the news of 1 keV electrons created interest but not value for the company.

[Zenithon AI raises $10 million to develop AI models for extreme physics](https://www.kings.cam.ac.uk/news/start-backed-kings-e-lab-raises-10m). Founded by [Alex Higginbottom](https://www.linkedin.com/in/alex-higginbottom-049056141/) and [Abetharan Antony](https://www.linkedin.com/in/abetharan-antony/), the company is building physics-based AI world models for fusion reactors, rockets, and semiconductor manufacturing.

[The 1 Cent Fusion Energy project concludes, and its founders move on to Borealis Fusion](https://1cf.energy/1cfe-completed/). A major conclusion that the non-fusion core components alone, under their assumptions, would cost 2.9 cents/kWh. Availability and financing were among the largest cost drivers. Fusion fuel was also important because of the impacts of what blanket and breeding systems are needed. They've made their [source code](https://github.com/1cFE) available on github. After doing this study, two members are going on to found [Borealis Fusion](https://borealisfusion.com/) to do p-B11 fusion.

## Supply chain, manufacturing, and enabling technology

[CFS places the largest ever order (>10,000 kilometers) of HTS tape with Fujikura for ARC](https://www.fujikura.co.jp/en/news/pressrelease/20260930cfsarc1km.html). This is about the same amount used in [SPARC](https://blog.cfs.energy/how-cfs-is-building-a-fusion-factory-not-just-a-single-fusion-machine/) and half that needed for [ARC](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/27830/chapter/6). At today's prices, that's a few $100M.

[Avalanche Energy selects Washington's Tri-Cities region for its manufacturing facility](https://www.avalanchefusion.com/news-release/avalanche-energy-and-tridec-advance-plans-for-first-global-fusion-machine-manufacturing-facility-in-the-tri-cities). 

[DIII-D and Kyoto Fusioneering begin developing a divertor for negative-triangularity plasmas](https://kyotofusioneering.com/en/news/2026/10/08/4758). Nice they're pushing this forward, but looking at the tiny plasma cross section needed to make the negative-triangularity plasma in DIII-D makes it seem like a square peg in a round hole situation.

[Larmor mmWave Systems introduces its high-power microwave business](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/larmor_the-fusion-industry-needs-a-spark-plug-activity-7511450675839442944-5jGC). The EPFL Swiss Plasma Center spinout, co-founded by [Massimo Carpita](https://www.linkedin.com/in/massimo-carpita/) and [Mackenzie van Rossem](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mackvr/), aims to commercialize high-power, high-frequency microwave systems.

## Government, regulation, and public programs

[Germany launches a €500 million milestone-based fusion development program](https://www.sprind.org/en/words/magazine/nuclear-fusion-milestone-program). The Federal Agency for Breakthrough Innovation (SPRIND) will administer the program, with €500 million to be made available by 2029 and up to €2.5 billion in longer-term  funding. Like the US program, multiple companies will compete for funding tied to technical and financial milestones with independent evaluations. 

[The U.S.-Japan Technology Prosperity Deal highlights fusion](https://www8.cao.go.jp/cstp/kokusaiteki/nichibei/joint_statement_tpd.pdf). 

[DOE's Genesis Mission announces award to build a SPARC digital twin](https://www.energy.gov/articles/energy-department-announces-new-genesis-mission-awards-advance-super-intelligence-science). The project targets full-shot predictions in less than 20 minutes, fast enough to inform decisions between experimental shots, with the broader goal of improving SPARC operation and ARC design.
