# Fusion Fortnightly | 2026-10-12

No fluff, all facts: Type One Energy raises <span>$</span>200 million. General Fusion reaches 1 keV on LM26. Germany launches a €500 million fusion milestone program. CFS orders more than 10,000 km of superconducting tape for ARC. Helical Fusion raises another ¥3.23 billion. STEP unveils its latest power plant design.

## Companies, financing, and commercial development

[Type One Energy closes a <span>$</span>200 million Series B](https://typeoneenergy.com/insights-announcements/type-one-energy-completes-200m-series-b-financing/). Total investment in Type One now exceeds <span>$</span>400 million. The funding supports Infinity One, its engineering prototype in Tennessee, but certainly isn't enough to get to first plasma.

[TAE+TMTG file SEC Form S-4](https://www.sec.gov/ix?doc=/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000143774926031534/djt20260922_s4.htm). This is the long awaited first public details on a lot of interesting stuff on the potential merger and public trading of TAE. TAE had an August 2024 pay-to-play recapitalization: all existing preferred shares were converted to common. Investors that put more money into the new Series 12 round could restore a proportionate amount of their old preferred and received warrants at <span>$</span>0.01 per share. That gave investors an incentive to keep funding the company when TAE was under significant financial pressure. While the company was under high financial pressure, CEO Michl Binderbauer’s compensation increased sharply. Following a 15% pay cut in 2023 to save funds, his base salary rose from <span>$</span>512,000 to <span>$</span>1.1 million in May 2026, with a bonus up to 120%. He will get a <span>$</span>500,000 merger bonus and additional restricted stock units. TAE had only <span>$</span>114 million on June 30, 2026 and spent <span>$</span>184 million in its latest fiscal year. The TMTG merger and financing was critical important to continued experiments and company existance. TMTG advanced <span>$</span>200 million to TAE and committed up to another <span>$</span>100 million with a 7% note. 

The earlier roadmap predicted net energy in 2030 and net power 2031. This has now moved to: construction begins in 2026, first plasma in 2029, demonstrate fusion energy in 2031, and initial operations “thereafter.” This filing also has TAE very open on the technical risks: the confinement scaling needs to continue to much higher temperatures and densities, controlling a fuel mixture containing particles of very different masses is challenging, and the beam performance, beam–plasma coupling, helium confinement, impurity control, and conversion efficiencies are all hard as well.

There's also some interesting negotiation detail. TAE wanted to protect its shareholders against changes in the TMTG share price. It also sought protection on declines in TMTG’s stock and cryptocurrency holdings. TMTG removed those protections. TAE attempted to restore some of them. TMTG removed them again. TAE also proceeded without a fairness opinion on the merger for its own shareholders. Barclays said it lacked the TMTG projections needed to do its financial analysis and give an opinion. TAE’s board decided against seeking another adviser’s opinion. TAE explicitly mentioned the TMTG connection as useful with regulators. Its board’s rationale includes the company’s name recognition, directors, executives, major shareholders and their beneficiaries as sources of relationships and regulatory credibility and experience.

[TAE+TMTG files SEC Form 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1849635/000143774926031535/0001437749-26-031535-index.htm). It gives TMTG voting on TAE shareholders based on the value of the loan it's given to TAE even before the merger is complete.

[General Fusion reports LM26 plasma temperatures above 1 keV](https://generalfusion.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Thomson-Scattering-Measurement-of-1-keV-Electron-Temperature-on-Lawson-Machine-26.pdf). Lawson Machine 26 achieved electron temperatures exceeding 1 keV (12 million °C) through compression, with meaningful ion heating also reported. The results were co-published with UKAEA and measured using Thomson scattering diagnostics. This is a significant intermediate milestone for General Fusion's magnetized target fusion approach, though still short of its next goals of 10 keV and satisfying the Lawson criterion. It does not establish fusion breakeven or net energy production.

[General Fusion measures electron temperatures above 1 keV on LM26](https://generalfusion.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Thomson-Scattering-Measurement-of-1-keV-Electron-Temperature-on-Lawson-Machine-26.pdf). Thomson scattering measured a peak local electron temperature of 1.09 keV, up from 0.72 keV reported in July, aided by a new diagnostic view closer to the plasma core. A [companion paper](https://generalfusion.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Compressional-heating-above-1-keV-on-the-LM26-magnetized-target-fusion-machine.pdf) reports a later peak of 1.18 keV from AXUV measurements and neutron-inferred ion heating from 323 eV to 459 eV. The improved performance is attributed to better electron energy confinement, although plasma temperature still collapses at a compression ratio of 2.7. See the [Fusion Conclusion's July claim check](https://www.fusionconclusion.com/Claim-Check-2026-07-02/) for analysis of the earlier results. An interesting first data point for public market reactions to company-defined milestones: [GFUZ](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/GFUZ/?guccounter=1&guce_referrer=aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cuZ29vZ2xlLmNvbS8&guce_referrer_sig=AQAAAEzLBq6SmJDjFniMHH97hw_68ThzDEEi4pHutBcCuQlZjMGM-1VlBB3Hf6TEAlbW_akNGsZHJqeFCS4uDbKjFDHEZa_eeM5zihv4KbYwGa9IO1f7rjbb_YLSq7iHY4a09RFZRyoP2LU4aOrLFN5uLrk5sOp3AdAV2OUwwg1rN0qv) trading volume increased 5-10 times the previous weeks and the price transiently went up about 10% but is still well within the scatter of the last month or so. It looks like the news of 1 keV electrons created interest but not value for the company.

[The 1 Cent Fusion Energy project concludes, and its founders move on to Borealis Fusion](https://1cf.energy/1cfe-completed/). Fusion fuel was also important because of the impacts of what blanket and breeding systems are needed. They've made their [source code](https://github.com/1cFE) available on github. After doing this study, two members are going on to found [Borealis Fusion](https://borealisfusion.com/) to do p-B11 fusion.

## Government, regulation, and public programs

[Germany launches a €500 million milestone-based fusion development program](https://www.sprind.org/en/words/magazine/nuclear-fusion-milestone-program). The Federal Agency for Breakthrough Innovation (SPRIND) will administer the program, with €500 million to be made available by 2029 and up to €2.5 billion in longer-term  funding. Like the US program, multiple companies will compete for funding tied to technical and financial milestones with independent evaluations. 

[The U.S.-Japan Technology Prosperity Deal highlights fusion](https://www8.cao.go.jp/cstp/kokusaiteki/nichibei/joint_statement_tpd.pdf). 

[DOE's Genesis Mission announces award to build a SPARC digital twin](https://www.energy.gov/articles/energy-department-announces-new-genesis-mission-awards-advance-super-intelligence-science). The project targets full-shot predictions in less than 20 minutes, fast enough to inform decisions between experimental shots, with the broader goal of improving SPARC operation and ARC design.
